Yaz Media is a small, senior team of cinematographers, editors, and producers based in Los Angeles — obsessed with turning real moments into films that last.

Our Story

It started with one camera
and one unforgettable wedding

Yaz Media began when we filmed a friend's wedding in Santa Barbara and watched the whole room cry during the screening. That day set the standard we still hold: real emotion, beautifully captured, honestly edited.

Today we're a full-service production company. The same hands that cut a couple's highlight film on Monday direct a hotel commercial on Tuesday and color-grade a music video on Friday. Every project gets senior talent — we don't hand your story to interns.

What We Stand For

Three things we never compromise

Honest moments

We film what actually happens — the nervous laugh, the squeeze of a hand. Posed perfection is easy; real emotion is priceless.

Cinema-grade craft

Cinema cameras, real lighting, DaVinci color, and sound that feels like the room. Your film should hold up on a projector and a phone.

Calm, kind crews

Our teams blend in, stay quiet, and put people at ease. The best footage comes from days that feel effortless.
